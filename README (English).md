# <h1 align="center"> ABOUT THE PROJECT

## OBJECTIVE 
<p align="justify">Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to support CPTM's Logistics Management (GOL) in managing inventory of materials and railway maintenance tools. The project aims to integrate and process data from the ALVO ERP (RIOSOFT) through a structured ETL process, transforming it into management indicators such as inventory turnover, coverage, ABC Curve classification, and stockout or excess alerts. The solution's purpose is to offer greater visibility into inventory behavior, supporting faster, safer, and data-driven logistics decisions.</p>

## EXPECTED RESULT
<p align="justify">The project will deliver an interactive Power BI dashboard, fed by processed data from the ALVO ERP, with modules covering inventory overview, time evolution, financial indicators, inventory turnover, ABC Curve, and risk alerts (stockout, excess, and obsolescence). The result also includes technical documentation of the data model aimed at future corporate integration, as well as a consolidated management dashboard with the main strategic KPIs to support the decision-making of CPTM's logistics team.</p>

# SPRINT SCHEDULE

*Legend: ✅ Completed · ⬜ Planned*

## 📌 SPRINT 0 – ASSIGNMENT DEFINITION AND PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING
- ✅ Product Backlog Creation
- ✅ Alignment Meetings
- ✅ GitHub Repository Setup
- ✅ Task Definition in Jira Software
- ✅ Assignment of Activities to Members

## 📌 SPRINT 1 – DELIVERY ON OCTOBER 2
- ⬜ As an inventory manager, process and clean the ALVO ERP data, ensuring consistency and reliability in the analyses.
- ⬜ As a project manager, structure the GitHub repository to ensure traceability and versioning of artifacts.
- ⬜ As a data analyst, model the database with the movement history to perform time-based analyses of inflows and outflows.
- ⬜ As an inventory manager, view the total number of items, total value in stock, and average inventory on the dashboard.
- ⬜ As a dashboard user, apply filters by warehouse, period, material type, and cost center.
- ⬜ As a logistics analyst, track the time evolution of inventory and the history of inflows and outflows.
- ⬜ As an end user, navigate an intuitive and accessible interface with as few clicks as possible.
- ⬜ As a logistics analyst, view inventory turnover indicators by material (high and low turnover).
- ⬜ As a financial manager, check the average dwell time of stored materials.
- ⬜ As a financial manager, view the automated ABC Curve classification by criticality and budget impact.

## 📌 SPRINT 2 – DELIVERY ON OCTOBER 30
- ⬜ As a financial manager, view the ranking of materials with the highest financial impact.
- ⬜ As a supply analyst, view alerts for stagnant stock, excess stock, and stockout risk.
- ⬜ As a logistics analyst, identify materials at risk of obsolescence and bottlenecks in the replenishment process.
- ⬜ As an inventory planner, check the coverage time in days for each item.
- ⬜ As a logistics analyst, view predictive consumption analyses.
- ⬜ As a logistics analyst, track logistics efficiency indicators.
- ⬜ As a CPTM client, view the dashboard's evolution with the adjustments from Delivery 1 implemented.
- ⬜ As a manager, navigate a structured, consolidated BI dashboard with a professional look.
- ⬜ As an end user, access the platform responsively across different screens and devices.

## 📌 SPRINT 3 – DELIVERY ON NOVEMBER 27
- ⬜ As CPTM's IT team, receive the technical documentation of the data model for future integration with the ALVO ERP.
- ⬜ As an executive manager, navigate a consolidated management dashboard with the main strategic KPIs.
- ⬜ As a client and evaluator, watch the demo video and follow the presentation at the Solutions Fair.
